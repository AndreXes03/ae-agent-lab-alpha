# KYNEM motion demo

[Watch/download the native AE promotional film](https://github.com/AndreXes03/ae-agent-lab-alpha/releases/download/v0.1.0-alpha.6/KYNEM-README-demo-v18-clean.mp4).

The film shows “Animate MOVE”, a title and underline, an illustrative timeline, three layouts and the KYNEM closing. An agent created and revised native text, shapes, keyframes, effect controls and parenting through the local bridge, with human art direction and review. The command cards and timeline are designed graphics. This is a retimed promotional animation with authored native output, not a live execution recording or a prompt-to-video speed benchmark.

## Evidence and scope

Native layers/properties were inspected in After Effects 2026 (26.5) on one Mac. The master composition includes directed retiming. The selected film is the clean v18 export; the poster comes from that same video. The clean export is 1920×1080, 30 fps, 14.37 seconds, without audio. The MP4 is a release asset, kept out of Git history.

The film demonstrates the authored result. It does not establish that every step was automatic, that a single prompt reproduces the whole sequence, or that the workflow is verified on other Macs or AE versions.
